Onboarding Option A · revised on the research

Quiet Invite

Zero-account solo start — the app never asks for identity to open. "Invite your team" is a primary sidebar item on every screen (moved out of the footer: footer placements get 0.05–0.46% CTR), with a second contextual entry in the Assignee field. Identity is asked only at the click, and the modal says where agent sessions live and that self-hosting is on the roadmap. A milestone nudge is shown as a labelled post-launch fast-follow. The login is where it belongs: nowhere for a solo user; a one-time system-browser sign-in (GitHub, Google, or an email link — never a password) at the invite click, because a hosted workspace needs a real owner. Invitees sign in the same way from the join link.
